## Brand & Style

### Personality & Positioning
The design system powers an enterprise-grade AI intelligence environment tailored for public procurement oversight officers and compliance directors on high-scale government exchanges. The aesthetic prioritizes absolute computational authority, clinical precision, and quiet efficiency. Borrowing the disciplined, monospaced-adjacent restraint of engineering tools like Linear combined with the focused cognitive clarity of modern conversational AI interfaces, it eliminates decorative noise in favor of deep structural clarity.

### Aesthetic Movement
Modern Technical Minimalism meets Tonal Layering. The interface feels etched from dark obsidian glass and matte carbon, using razor-sharp 1px dividers, restrained corner geometry, micro-glows, and strict typographic hierarchy to manage dense legal, financial, and regulatory data streams without visual fatigue.

### Emotional Resonance
The experience communicates uncompromised scrutiny, systemic impartiality, and absolute confidence. Officers must feel equipped with an analytical defense instrument that turns opaque procurement bids into audit-grade verdicts in milliseconds.

## Colors

### Canvas & Surface Architecture
The color palette establishes an uncompromising pitch-black floor, elevating information through delicate surface stepped luminances:
- **Base Canvas (`bg-canvas`):** `#050505` — Deep background ground.
- **Root Screen (`bg-base`):** `#080808` — Main application workspace background.
- **Surface Level 1 (`bg-surface-subtle`):** `#0B0B0D` — Recessed panels, sidebars, query input shells.
- **Surface Level 2 (`bg-surface`):** `#101012` — Standard workspace cards, audit trail panels, data modules.
- **Surface Level 3 (`bg-surface-raised`):** `#141416` — Hover containers, active inspect drawers, elevated modals.
- **Surface Level 4 (`bg-surface-overlay`):** `#18181B` — Popovers, contextual command menus, floating tooltips.

### Structural Delimiters & Dividers
Borders serve as spatial anchors instead of heavy dividers:
- **Subtle Hairline (`border-subtle`):** `#1F1F23` (1px solid) — Internal structural dividers and tabular rows.
- **Standard Border (`border-default`):** `#242428` (1px solid) — Card boundaries, interactive field outlines.
- **Prominent Border (`border-strong`):** `#2C2C30` (1px solid) — Modal envelopes, focus rings, selected card peripheries.

### Typography Contrast Spectrum
- **Primary Text:** `#F4F4F5` — Critical verdicts, numerical metrics, table headers, document titles.
- **Secondary Text:** `#A1A1AA` — Metadata fields, system commentary, officer notes, navigational items.
- **Muted / Tertiary Text:** `#71717A` — Timestamps, clause references, disabled actions, placeholder markers.

### Accent System: Lavender Matrix
The violet-lavender spectrum is deployed with surgical reserve. It is never applied for ambient decoration; it signifies active analytical focus, primary confirmation, and AI synthesis:
- **Primary Accent (`accent-primary`):** `#8B7CF6` — Primary interactive buttons, selection outlines, active radio indices.
- **Highlight Accent (`accent-secondary`):** `#A78BFA` — AI extraction callouts, inline token highlights, parser cues.
- **Muted Lavender Tint (`accent-tint`):** `rgba(139, 124, 246, 0.08)` — Subtle interactive card fills, highlighted table rows.

### Status & Verification Tokens
Engineered specifically for government compliance determination:
- **Compliant (`status-compliant`):** `#10B981` (emerald); background: `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`; border: `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`.
- **Non-Compliant (`status-violation`):** `#F43F5E` (rose); background: `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.08)`; border: `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.2)`.
- **Review Required (`status-warning`):** `#F59E0B` (amber); background: `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08)`; border: `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.2)`.
- **Insufficient Evidence (`status-neutral`):** `#71717A` (zinc); background: `rgba(113, 113, 122, 0.1)`; border: `rgba(113, 113, 122, 0.25)`.

## Typography

### Hierarchy Implementation
- **Headlines & Structural Titles (Geist):** Set with tight negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.025em`) to evoke technical competence and crisp layout density.
- **Body & Clause Prose (Inter):** Formatted for prolonged reading of intricate procurement specifications, RFP clauses, and vendor certifications. The 13px base size reflects high-density enterprise dashboard norms while retaining high legibility.
- **Labels, Badges, & Metrics (Geist):** Tracked slightly wide (`+0.02em` to `+0.04em`) and uppercase where applicable to deliver rapid visual parsing in audit tables.
- **Reference & Hash Tokens (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for tender IDs, SHA-256 integrity signatures, GeM vendor codes, and clause sub-sections (e.g., `Cl. 4.12.A`).

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Architecture
A fluid, density-optimized layout designed primarily for wide enterprise consoles (1440px to 2560px) while adapting down to compact inspection laptops:
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column variable grid with fixed 64px collapsed / 240px expanded navigation rail, 380px contextual AI side-car review panel, and fluid central analysis canvas. Gutter: 24px.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. The AI side-car converts into an overlaid slide-over drawer triggered by contextual verdict nodes. Gutter: 16px.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column single-stream stack for urgent field reviews, escalation signing, and rapid status triage. Gutter: 12px.

### Density Rhythm
Vertical and horizontal rhythm operates on a base 4px sub-grid, with 8px and 12px increments governing micro-component spacing (input paddings, badge gutters, table cell heights). Standard dense table rows enforce a rigorous 36px or 44px fixed height to accommodate maximum audit visibility without scrolling excess.

## Elevation & Depth

### Tonal Stratification
Depth is conveyed not through heavy drop shadows, but through deliberate value separation of layered surfaces and razor-sharp border luminescence:
- **Ground Floor:** `#050505` to `#080808` represents the infinite root plane.
- **Docked Panels & Structural Panes:** `#0B0B0D` bound by `1px solid #1F1F23`.
- **Active Data Cards:** `#101012` outlined by `1px solid #242428`.

### Ambient Glows & Shadows
Traditional drop shadows are avoided. Instead, floating elements deploy black ambient occlusions paired with microscopic lavender highlights:
- **Raised Module Overlay:** `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75), 0px 0px 0px 1px #2C2C30`.
- **Command Palette & AI Modals:** `0px 16px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.9), 0px 0px 0px 1px #2C2C30, 0px 0px 24px 0px rgba(139, 124, 246, 0.06)`.
- **Active Focus Field:** `0px 0px 0px 1px #8B7CF6, 0px 0px 12px -2px rgba(139, 124, 246, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

### Corner Curvature Language
The geometric form factor is restrained, structural, and modern:
- **Default Elements (Inputs, Buttons, Badges, Table Rows):** `6px` (`rounded-sm` / `rounded-md`).
- **Cards, Panels, & Code Blocks:** `8px` (`rounded-lg`).
- **Modal Dialogues & Flyout Panels:** `10px` (`rounded-xl`).
- **Pill Exceptions:** Fully rounded (`9999px`) is reserved exclusively for numerical count indicators and circular officer identity avatars.
